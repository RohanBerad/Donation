Get Support

How To Get Help

If you or someone you know is facing a medical emergency, treatment cost, or crisis, we are here to help. Follow the steps below to submit a request and our team will review it within 48 hours.

Step 1

Prepare your documents and fill out the help request form on our homepage.

Step 2

Our team verifies your details and reviews the submitted documents.

Step 3

Once approved, we publish the campaign and start raising funds for you.

Required Documents

To help us verify your request quickly, please prepare the following documents. You can upload them directly through the help request form on the homepage.

  • 1
    Medical Report / Diagnosis

    Doctor's letter, hospital discharge summary, or pathology report confirming the condition.

  • 2
    Treatment Cost Estimate

    An official estimate or invoice from the hospital/clinic showing the required amount.

  • 3
    Government-issued ID

    Aadhaar card, passport, or any valid photo ID to confirm identity.

  • 4
    Address Proof

    Utility bill, ration card, or any document showing your current residence.

  • 5
    Bank Account Details

    A cancelled cheque or bank passbook copy so funds can be transferred directly to the treatment account.

What Happens Next?

Review Timeline

We review every submission within 48 hours. You will receive an email update once your request is approved or if we need more information.

Campaign Creation

Approved requests are turned into live campaigns on our website. Donors can then contribute directly to your funding goal.

Fund Disbursement

Funds are paid directly to the hospital or treatment provider. We do not transfer cash to individuals to ensure transparency.

Updates & Follow-up

Our team stays in touch to track treatment progress and share updates with donors who supported the campaign.
