Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you donate, register for an account, or contact us, we collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, and donation details. We never collect or store your full card or bank details -- payments are handled through our payment flow, and only a transaction reference is saved against your donation.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process donations, send receipts, keep you updated on the campaigns you've supported, and respond to messages you send us through the Contact page. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Cookies

We use session cookies to keep you signed in to your donor dashboard and to remember items like your admin session. These cookies do not track you across other websites.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Access to donor and donation records in our admin panel is restricted to authorized staff accounts only.

5. Your Rights

You can review or update your profile information at any time from your donor dashboard, or contact us to request that your account and associated data be deleted.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact page or email us at {{ site_settings.contact_email }}.
